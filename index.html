# Privacy Policy — Loan Payment Calculator **Last updated: November 10, 2026** Loan Payment Calculator is a mobile application that helps users calculate loan payments, repayment periods, and related financial information. This privacy policy explains how the application handles user information. ## Information Collection and Use Loan Payment Calculator does not collect, transmit, or store personal information. All loan calculations are performed locally on your device. Information entered into the application, such as loan amounts, interest rates, and repayment periods, is used solely to perform the requested calculations. This information is not transmitted to the developer or any third party. ## Data Sharing Loan Payment Calculator does not share user information with third parties. The application does not use advertising services, analytics services, or user tracking technologies. ## Data Storage and Retention The application does not maintain user accounts or store loan information on external servers. No personal data is retained by the developer. Information entered for calculations is processed locally on the device. ## Data Security Because loan calculations are performed locally and no user information is transmitted to the developer, the application does not require users to submit personal information to external services. ## Children's Privacy Loan Payment Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. ## Changes to This Privacy Policy This privacy policy may be updated if the application's features or data handling practices change.
